## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes **retail sales data** from the Superstore dataset to uncover:  
- Profit-draining categories  
- Regional sales & profitability  
- Monthly sales trends  
- Inventory optimization opportunities  

The project combines **SQL for data analysis** and **Tableau for visualization** to deliver actionable business insights.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
✔️ Identify **profitable vs loss-making categories & sub-categories**  
✔️ Analyze **sales and profit trends across regions**  
✔️ Study **monthly sales patterns** (seasonality, holiday peaks)  
✔️ Provide **recommendations for inventory & sales strategies**  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source:** Kaggle – Superstore Sales Data  
- **Size:** ~10,000+ records  
- **Fields include:** Order Date, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Profit  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies  
- **SQL (MySQL Workbench):** Data cleaning & analysis  
- **Tableau Public:** Interactive dashboards  
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data preparation & validation  

---

## ⚙️ Workflow  

### 🔹 Data Preparation  
- Imported dataset (`superstore_sales.csv`) into MySQL  
- Cleaned nulls & duplicates  
- Standardized column types & date formats  

### 🔹 Data Analysis (SQL)  
- Total **sales & profit by region**  
- Profitability by **category & sub-category**  
- Monthly sales trends  

### 🔹 Data Visualization (Tableau)  
- 📊 Sales & Profit by Region  
- 📉 Category/Sub-Category Profitability  
- 📆 Monthly Sales Trends  
- 🎛️ Interactive filters (Category, Region)  

---

## 📊 Key Insights  
- 🌎 **West region** generated the most profit  
- 💻 **Technology category** was the most profitable  
- 🪑 **Furniture category** showed losses → optimization needed  
- 📈 **Sales peaked in Nov–Dec** (holiday season effect)
